# Production and product design

## Inquiry

What turns a device model into a dependable product with an explicit resource, quality and failure contract?

## Intended reader and use

Technical and product readers delivering Edge AI applications.

Learning or decision goal: Translate model capability into a reviewable product contract, artifact lifecycle and measured device workload.

## Scope and time boundary

Model packaging and compatibility; download and availability; update and rollback; telemetry; privacy; offline UX; resources, deadlines and product value.

Evidence boundary: Current official device platform and model distribution documentation plus primary security guidance inspected through 2026-10-12.

## Exclusions

No model downloads, execution of upstream code, hardware measurements, confidential workplace examples, vendor rankings, legal assurances or external publication. Model architecture and runtime detail belongs in sibling pages.

## Deliverables

- Repository page `src/content/docs/edge-ai/production-product-design.mdx` is the canonical narrative.
- Source, claim, run and visual records support the narrative.
- Counterevidence, uncertainty and actual review results remain explicit.

## Output contract

- Kind: site-page
- Canonical path: src/content/docs/edge-ai/production-product-design.mdx
- Reading depth: A focused mechanism explanation with a worked scenario, tradeoffs and practical next links.
- Structure: Practical problem, mechanisms, worked system, tradeoffs and bounded guidance.
- Citation style: Native claim-local Markdown footnotes.
- Evidence cutoff: 2026-10-12, established only after inspection.
- Refresh expectation: Recheck mutable platform availability and API behavior before relying on them.
- Distribution and privacy boundary: Public-safe repository and output.

## Visual contract

One independent mechanism flow built with the existing FocusCanvas and ModelFlow; complete visible and exported text, numbered figure anchor and cited synthesis caption. No reproduced artwork is required to explain the system boundary. Record component source and page anchor.

## Research map

- Evidence dimensions and resolving sources: Artifact identity and rollout; runtime availability and compatibility; resource and failure UX; privacy and observability.
- Skeptical direction: An apparently compatible model can change tokenization or semantics; offline availability may fail after eviction; a benchmark can miss thermal throttling, concurrency, download and failure experience.

## Budget and checkpoints

Two bounded evidence passes per page, then a separate skeptical review, source-level writing review and research validation. Parent coordinates site validation, actual browser review and agent exports; leave these delivery gates pending until performed.

## Success criteria

Opening explains the user problem and mechanism; one concrete hypothetical scenario exposes assumptions; consequential claims have inspected sources; no fabricated measurement; figure teaches a distinct relationship and exports fully; skeptics and evidence limits are visible. The parent completes site validation, rendering and exports before final delivery.
