# High agency

## Inquiry

What is high agency, why does it matter in the AI era, and how can people develop it through evidence-informed practice?

## Intended reader and use

Curious adults who want to take useful initiative in work, learning and everyday projects, including people using AI tools. This is the first substantive Human Capabilities page in Latent Light.
