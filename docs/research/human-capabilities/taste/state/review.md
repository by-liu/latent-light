## Separate skeptical evidence pass

The local challenge pass checked the source/claim map before choosing the page's
central argument. It rejected the proposed leap from contemporary discussion to
measured popularity growth. Axios supplies only a brief attention claim; the
commercial trends report supplies self-described practice and predictions.

Taste is not treated as one validated psychological trait. Hume and Graham are
arguments by practitioners/philosophers, Leder and Reber are models/reviews,
and Tai is a conceptual education synthesis. The paper's 2004 issue date is kept
separate from Wiley's 2010 digitization. Hume's historical assumptions and
Bourdieu's account of social classification are not endorsed as universal rankings.
The Bourdieu citation is the inspected primary excerpt, not the university's
secondary summary or a claim that the entire book was inspected.

Shared aesthetic judgment differs by domain in Vessel's experiments. That cannot
establish culture-independent standards. Salganik's social-influence manipulation
concerns downloads in a particular artificial music market; its quality proxy is
independent-condition popularity, not objective artistic excellence. Bellaiche's
label experiment used AI-generated paintings under randomized purported-creator
labels. It isolates a labeling effect, not comparative superiority of human art
or an argument that provenance should never matter.

Peer-assessment findings concern academic performance, not general taste. Many
included designs confound giving and receiving critique and have clustering and
measurement limitations. Perceptual learning and intuition research provide
bounded inputs to original practice suggestions. No assembled programme or
cross-domain taste improvement is claimed to have been tested.

Doshi and Hauser's story experiment concerns GPT-4 starting ideas for eight-
sentence stories, not universal AI creativity. P&G's published analysis includes
791 workshop completers, with five ideas in all conditions. More highly rated
selected ideas coexisted with poorer best-idea selection. The page must retain
both facts and avoid presenting the authors' suggested mechanisms as isolated
causal results.

The Scientific Taste preprint is version 3 from August 19, with 720,341 pairs,
not the earlier search snippet's rounded 700K. We will omit that quantity unless
it adds explanatory value. Its citation labels are imperfect proxies and its
ideation results rely mainly on model evaluators, with no experimental validation
of generated ideas. TASTE's 77% is estimated human agreement with filtered labels,
not an objective ceiling of taste. It has 92 dependent pairs drawn from 50
proposals and ten researchers. Its best tested model's 60% cannot be compared
numerically with a different scientific-preference benchmark. Narrow learnability
and narrow failure together do not establish a permanent human monopoly or a
job-protection guarantee.
