# Selection audit

The user's request is for well recognized models, not a complete registry.
Recognition has no universal threshold. This selection is an editorial reading
map of established families and consequential research contrasts, not a measured
popularity ranking. Primary launch statements establish dates, not influence.

The audit adds 15 entries. GLM is represented by its 2021 paper, ChatGLM-6B,
GLM-4.5, and the GLM-5 paper. These are distinct milestones, not synonyms.
Llama 2, o1-preview, Gemini 1.5 Pro and Kimi K2 fill historical gaps.
Qwen2.5 and Qwen3 restore language-family coverage beside its vision models.
Gemma 3 and InternVL improve compact and multimodal coverage. DeepSeek-V2
adds a meaningful precursor; Mamba adds an alternative sequence architecture.
Grok 4 adds a missing established proprietary family.

Do not add every GLM-5.x, GPT, Claude, Qwen, Kimi or Llama checkpoint. Smaller
variants, coder fine tunes, task-specific models and benchmark-only forks are
omitted unless they would change the architectural reading path. Falcon, OPT,
DBRX, Phi, ERNIE and other recognizable families remain possible future
additions, not claims of unimportance. The figure cannot be a complete history.

## Added entries and editorial reasons

- GLM: Established bilingual language model family and distinct pretraining research.
- ChatGLM-6B: Early locally deployable bilingual chat model; its retrospective report documents substantial community use.
- GLM-4.5: Selected later GLM family milestone; no promotional ranking is repeated.
- GLM-5: Representative 2026 paper from an established family, not a claim that this is its newest checkpoint.
- Llama 2: Major early public family release, with partner distribution documented at launch.
- Mamba: Recognizable alternative sequence modeling architecture; complements the existing architecture collection.
- InternVL: Established VLM family; fills a multimodal rather than another chat-model gap.
- Gemini 1.5 Pro: Distinct long context generation in an established multimodal family.
- DeepSeek-V2: Provides the architectural precursor to the already selected V3 and R1 milestones.
- OpenAI o1-preview: Recognizable reasoning model milestone rather than a minor GPT variant.
- Qwen2.5: Widely used language family milestone that balances the previous emphasis on its vision variants.
- Gemma 3: Representative compact multimodal family with documented distribution and ecosystem use.
- Qwen3: Representative language and thinking milestone in an established family.
- Grok 4: Recognizable major proprietary family; adds missing coverage without every Grok generation.
- Kimi K2: Representative agentic MoE model preceding the already listed K3.

## Event boundary checks

ChatGLM-6B's retrospective report explicitly states the March 14, 2023 release
in Section 1 and Table 1. GLM-5 uses the February 17, 2026 paper date, not an
unverified February 11 first-release assertion. InternVL's preprint is dated
December 21, 2023. DeepSeek-V2 uses May 7, 2024, its first preprint. The Kimi
K2 initial release is July 11, 2025, not the later 0905 update. o1-preview
is distinct from the full o1 model released later in 2024. Mamba is not a
Transformer; the map includes it as an architecture milestone.
