# deepseek-v3 technical deep dive

## Primary question

 what distinguishes this model, how do its defining mechanisms work, and what do the published training, inference, and evaluation disclosures establish?

## Intended reader and use

 technically curious learners with Transformer foundations. Goal: a transferable mental model, not reproduction instructions.

## User-supplied facts

 requests for deep dives, clear scale, purposeful figures, and iterative review. Model specifications are research findings, not user supplied facts.

## Scope and time boundary

 original generation and exact public representative checkpoint. Eligibility requires a substantive official technical report and identifiable public weights. Inspect report mechanisms, training, evaluations and relevant appendices; inspect official config, tokenizer metadata, licenses, and targeted code where available.

## Exclusions

Exclude later derivatives unless explicitly contrasted. No model execution, weight download, spending, private data, or broad impact claims. Evidence cutoff: actual inspection on October 10, 2026.

## Deliverables

 canonical report.md with nearby numbered footnotes, derivative MDX site page, public safe source and claim ledgers, run records and original technical flow visual.

## Report contract

Aim for a focused study rather than a fixed word quota. Refresh only if report or artifact changes; no automated schedule.

## Visual contract

Use an original numbered explanatory visual when it clarifies a mechanism, cost or branch. Preserve its full meaning in prose and a text equivalent. Source artwork is not reproduced. Parent reviews rendered mobile/theme/export output.

## Research map

1. Identity and openness: exact report revision, artifact commit and separate licenses.
2. Scale and architecture: representative dimensions, active versus total parameters, data units and compute accounting.
3. Distinctive mechanisms: motivation, computation, costs and supporting evidence.
4. Training and inference: reported stages, objective/reward separation, processing and state.
5. Skeptical review: confounds, missing disclosures, implementation/report mismatches, benchmark conditions and context distinctions.
6. Presentation: one useful original data flow, readable opening, selective numbers and depth.

## Success criteria

Consequential claims trace to inspected primary evidence; numbers and variants match; missing recipe and cost disclosures remain explicit; original diagrams have concise provenance and accessible textual equivalents. Source/number/causality review is separate from drafting. Structural research validation must pass. Parent integration owns complete site validation and browser/export/theme/mobile review. Do not attest these before integration.

## Budget and checkpoints

Bounded parallel portfolio task. Prioritize mechanism and evidence completeness over new release count. No placeholder site page if eligibility fails. Public repository records may contain only public safe metadata and synthesis. Downloaded PDFs stay outside repository unless redistribution rights are verified. Do not reproduce source artwork or substantial text. No commit, push or deploy.

## Assumptions and decisions

The original generation is the study identity even if the repository later gains derivatives. Missing disclosures remain unknown. Claims based on static artifact inspection are distinguished from report findings and no runtime results are asserted. Canonical headings follow the research protocol; site headings follow the model study template.


## Revision 4 visual contract supersedes the initial restriction

Prefer purposeful original report artwork with a documented basis: license, permission or an assessed applicable exception. Missing permissive terms alone do not require omission. Inspect embedded third-party material and exact figure scope; preserve copyright, attribution, version and crop records. Keep independent diagrams when they teach a different mechanism. This supersedes earlier original-only or explicit-license-only selection rules; it does not authorize full-paper redistribution. No commit, push, deployment or model execution.
