# Qwen3-VL technical study

## Primary question

What distinguishes Qwen3-VL, how does visual evidence enter its computation, and what do its public report and released artifacts establish?

## Intended reader and use

Readers with Transformer foundations seeking an accurate multimodal technical explanation. The learning goal is to trace one representative checkpoint from pixels to predictions and understand its distinctive design and evidence limits.

## Scope and time boundary

Public reports and appendices, official repositories, model cards, small configuration and processor artifacts. Selected evidence inspected October 10, 2026. Pin report versions and artifact revisions when retrieved.

## Exclusions

No checkpoint execution, weight download, paid compute, untrusted code execution, universal leaderboard ranking, or invented training recipes. This is not an audit of all family variants.

## Deliverables

Canonical report.md and derived MDX model page, one purposeful responsive original architecture visual, and inspected source, claim, run and visual ledgers.

## Report contract

Canonical Markdown, approximately 8 to 12 minutes of reading with optional deeper detail. Native claim-local footnotes. Public-safe writing and records. Refresh on request, not scheduled. Evidence cutoff October 10, 2026 for inspected artifacts only.

## Visual contract

One high-level input-to-output view after the opening. A second mechanism view only if it teaches a different relationship. Original code-native visual, not reproduced paper artwork. Captions Figure N, stable anchors, explicit synthesis provenance, and essential meaning in prose. Rendered presentation review remains pending until integration.

## Research map

1. Identity and public availability: report, official card, licenses and configuration.
2. Architecture and multimodal interface: report method, appendix and processor specification.
3. Training: stage-specific objectives, trainable modules, data counts, optimization, and compute disclosures.
4. Inference: preparation, representation, output and applicable cache or retrieval contract.
5. Evaluation and disconfirmation: central comparisons, controlled ablations, reported failures, confounds and absent disclosure.
6. Presentation: intuitive highlights, compact scale table, numbered visual, equations and public-safe footnotes.

## Budget and checkpoints

One complete pilot then parallel-family studies. Two evidence passes per model where needed: primary report/artifact mapping, then separate adversarial source and unit check. Do not spend time collecting redundant launch sources. Stop with material gaps explicitly stated rather than plausible details.

## Success criteria

The representative artifact is identified, consequential assertions have inspected source locators, a worked token example states assumptions, training and inference are not conflated, evaluations retain their conditions, and the narrative and original visual pass technical review. Full completion requires root integration checks for desktop/mobile, both themes, exports and build.

## User-supplied facts

The user requests important open-sourced model deep dives using the established guide and template, with purposeful visuals and repeated presentation review.

## Assumptions and decisions

Use public weights under their actual licenses rather than claim that all data and training code are open source. Models are chosen for their distinct lessons. Root agent owns navigation, shared components and browser validation.

## Revision 3 coverage audit

User requests a thorough check of figures and important mechanisms for every study. Compare the pinned report (including relevant appendices), targeted code and current page. Record a coverage matrix and figure selection decisions in coverage-audit.md. Add useful licensed source figures or independent diagrams, not a quota. Preserve variant, training/inference, evaluation and licensing boundaries. No commit, push, deployment or model execution. A second source-entailment pass and fresh site/visual/export checks are required.

## Revision 4 visual contract supersedes the initial restriction

Prefer purposeful original report artwork with a documented basis: license, permission or an assessed applicable exception. Missing permissive terms alone do not require omission. Inspect embedded third-party material and exact figure scope; preserve copyright, attribution, version and crop records. Keep independent diagrams when they teach a different mechanism. This supersedes earlier original-only or explicit-license-only selection rules; it does not authorize full-paper redistribution. No commit, push, deployment or model execution.
