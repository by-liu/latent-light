**Figure 2.** Three policy roles, one group reward signal. Independent explanatory diagram of the January report’s GRPO objective.[^grpo]

## Meaning

The old policy samples a group. Rewards produce normalized advantages. The current policy scores the sampled responses to form the current versus old ratio. A separate reference policy supplies the KL anchor. These terms update the current policy; the next iteration refreshes the old policy.
