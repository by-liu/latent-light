Which absent Qwen, GLM, Gemma, OpenAI and MiniMax public-weight generations add important technical lessons to the existing 25-study catalog?

Readers moving from Transformer foundations into released LLM/VLM systems. Explain distinctive mechanisms, actual training disclosure, inference state and evidence conditions, rather than collect release names.

## Scope and time boundary

Research cutoff October 10, 2026, only for sources actually inspected. Audit historical landmarks and newer report-backed generations in these five families. ## Exclusions

No weight/data downloads, model execution, compute provisioning, dependency changes, contact, commit, push or deployment. No closed API-only models as public-weight deep dives. No standalone page for every quantization or minor checkpoint update. No blanket inference that public weights imply a complete open-source AI system.

## Deliverables

Complete cited website studies; canonical Markdown per-model reports; public-safe sources/claims/visuals and coverage audit; useful original figures with precise edition/locator/crop and reuse basis; family sidebar and linked reading paths; tests, desktop/mobile/theme/focus and complete Markdown/MDX/agent export checks. Report reading depth follows each generation's disclosed complexity, not a word quota. Refresh is manual; no recurring jobs. Source reports remain outside public repo; website-ready bounded crops may enter public assets under the content standards.

## Research map

1. Selection: verify report identity, public artifact, date and distinct learning value.
2. Mechanics: read architecture, training, state, processor and targeted implementation.
3. Evidence: retain model/configuration, stage, metric, baseline and conditions of relevant ablations.
4. Presentation: purposeful original figures, exact selected-checkpoint scope, concise captions and readable page flow.
5. Skeptical pass: source/edition mismatch, undisclosed recipes, cross-variant numbers, benchmark/architecture confounds, licenses and third-party art.
6. Integration: stable family navigation, catalog coverage, regression checks and complete exports.

## Budget and checkpoints

Bounded family audit followed by primary-source passes and a separate evidence/presentation review. Stop searching when source coverage resolves the chosen study, not after accumulating a fixed number of links. Send findings at family selection, draft completion and final integration. Final checks pass or exact failures are reported. No false claims of runtime reproduction, rights grants or complete undisclosed recipe reconstruction.
