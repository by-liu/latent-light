# Personal AI Agents

## Inquiry

Compare personal AI services by delegated responsibility, external authority, human participation and verifiable completion.

## Intended reader and use

Curious builders and product-minded readers who want a concise, evidence-aware orientation, not investment or procurement advice.

## Scope and time boundary

Personal and household tasks with explicitly marked professional overlap; advertised services checked October 10, 2026. No external task execution or reliability trial.

## Exclusions

Company deep dives, hiring information, private career material, startup proposals, exhaustive catalogs, unsupported market rankings and confidential information.

One canonical MDX page, a cited generated overview, a complete searchable company directory, useful interpretation and unresolved questions. Internal records support later refreshes.
