## Exact generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a new standalone website icon concept for Latent Light, a calm personal notebook about AI, ideas, and human potential.
Primary request: create one original lightning-bolt icon inspired by the clean, instantly recognizable energy of the ⚡ emoji. It should communicate a flash of understanding.
Subject and shape: exactly one bold, compact, beautifully proportioned zigzag bolt. Upper tip points toward the upper right, lower tip toward the lower left. Broad substantial silhouette, a clear central zigzag, confident angular tips and very subtly softened inner corners. Make the bolt feel carefully drawn and distinctive through proportion rather than added symbols.
Style: refined minimal graphic logo, crisp vector-like edges, mostly flat. A restrained amber folded side facet along one edge gives the golden bolt a little depth; the whole mark must still read as one simple bolt at 16–32 pixels. Bright warm golden yellow with a slightly lighter upper face and deeper amber fold. Friendly, intelligent and energetic, suitable for a quiet editorial website.
Composition: square canvas, a single centered complete icon occupying about 78 percent of the height, balanced clear space around it. Truly transparent background.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no surrounding badge, no circle, no rounded-square container, no extra sparks, no stars, no orbit, no circuit traces, no lens flare, no glow, no cast shadow, no glossy plastic, no metallic texture, no photorealism, no 3D extrusion, no mockup, no grid, no multiple variations. Produce a usable isolated icon asset, not a presentation board.
```

## Exact refinement prompt

```text
Edit the supplied lightning icon. Preserve its exact overall zigzag silhouette, angle, proportions, centered position, and golden yellow with amber folded facets. Clean the artwork into a crisp minimal logo: remove every isolated yellow or red speck, stray pixel, rough fringe, and noisy mark outside the lightning bolt. All pixels outside the single continuous bolt must be fully transparent. Use smooth clean antialiased perimeter edges and broad smooth color faces without grain or mottling. Keep the shape bold and simple for a website favicon. Preserve a genuinely transparent background. No text, no added elements, no surrounding container, no shadow, no glow. Exactly one clean standalone icon.
```
