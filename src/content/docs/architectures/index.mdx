How does a model turn context into a useful answer? This section builds the
mental model behind large language models (LLMs) and vision language models
(VLMs), then follows the design choices that change how they process information.

## Choose a reading path

| Path | What it gives you | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | The intuition, mathematics, and data flow of a Transformer | [Transformer foundations](/architectures/foundations/) |
| Architectural Evolution | The problems behind alternative designs and their tradeoffs | [The design map](/architectures/evolution/) |
| Model Studies | Concrete examples of how the pieces fit together | [Reading model architectures](/architectures/models/) |

Start with Foundations if the mechanisms are unfamiliar. Otherwise, follow a
question: [what should a model remember](/architectures/evolution/attention-and-memory/),
[how should it use position](/architectures/evolution/position-and-long-context/),
[where should it spend computation](/architectures/evolution/ffns-and-experts/),
or [how should it read visual evidence](/architectures/evolution/multimodal-integration/)?

## Depth without an endless catalog

Foundations is a complete visual walkthrough of the core Transformer.
The evolution pages are focused mechanism explanations. Model studies connect
those ideas in [GPT-2](/architectures/models/gpt-2/) and
[Qwen3-VL](/architectures/models/qwen3-vl/), with each study stating what its
sources establish and what remains unverified.

Training and inference appear where they explain an architectural choice.
Standalone computer vision, robot policies, agent orchestration, and a full
post training curriculum are outside this section's current scope.

## Built to evolve

This is a learning map, not a ranking or a claim to cover every new release.
New work belongs here when it changes our understanding of a mechanism,
reveals a useful combination, or challenges an existing conclusion.

Sources sit beside the claims they support. Selected evidence dates describe
what was checked, not an exhaustive frontier search. For questions worth
investigating next, see [Emerging directions](/architectures/evolution/emerging-directions/).
