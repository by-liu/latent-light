The next useful direction is a question whose answer would change our
understanding. This page turns the model studies into a research agenda:
what to change, what to hold fixed, what to measure, and what could falsify
the explanation. It is not a forecast or a claim that these questions are
unsolved everywhere.

## Questions worth testing

| Direction | One controlled change | What would make it useful? | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Memory | Change recurrent/global ratio at a declared budget | Retrieval under distance and distractors, plus state and decoding cost | [Kimi Linear](/architectures/models/kimi-linear/), [MiniMax 01](/architectures/models/minimax-01/) |
| Selected history | Change selector or block budget | Preserve tasks requiring several distant facts; count index overhead and cache | [GLM-5](/architectures/models/glm-5/), [MiniMax M3](/architectures/models/minimax-m3/) |
| Capacity | Change expert width, count or sharing | Distinguish active work, total weights, communication and quality | [Mixtral](/architectures/models/mixtral/), [DeepSeek-V3](/architectures/models/deepseek-v3/) |
| Depth | Change residual reads, writes or source granularity | Improve final behavior without unstable optimization or excessive traffic | [Kimi K3](/architectures/models/kimi-k3/), [Qwen3.8-Next](/architectures/models/qwen3-8-flash-next/) |
| Visual evidence | Change resolution, merging or entry depth | Gains at matched visual and training budgets, including fine detail and conflicting evidence | [Qwen3-VL](/architectures/models/qwen3-vl/), [Gemma 3](/architectures/models/gemma-3/) |
| Position and reach | Change scaling or sampling while controlling evidence | Preserve use of the right region, frame or distant fact, not only accepted length | [Qwen2.5 1M](/architectures/models/qwen2-5-1m/), [Qwen2.5-VL](/architectures/models/qwen2-5-vl/) |

These are proposed comparisons. A matched active compute experiment and a
matched total parameter experiment answer different questions; choose and
state the contract rather than claim both were controlled.

## Negative results are part of the map

Qwen3.8-Next reports that a residual read shortcut can look acceptable in
pretraining metrics yet degrade later quality.[^qwen] MiniMax's sparse
attention report likewise shows that aggregate competitiveness does not mean
every long context subtask improves.[^msa]

Such evidence should narrow a claim. “Useful in this setup” is stronger
scientifically than an unsupported “better in general.” A failed replication
also needs its setup checked before declaring the mechanism false.

## What would change these pages?

A new study earns a place when it supplies a distinct mechanism, a useful
combination, an inspectable implementation, or counterevidence to an existing
explanation. Record the baseline, versions, task, budget and failure slices
beside the result. Connect it to the relevant mechanism page.

For a proprietary model, public disclosure and a plausible reconstruction
remain different evidence. An unknown internal detail must not become fact
by analogy with a released model.
