The next useful architecture is not necessarily the next release.
This page collects questions worth investigating and the evidence that would
make their answers useful. It is an editorial research agenda, not a forecast
or a claim that these questions are unsolved everywhere.

## Questions and tests

| Direction | Question to investigate | A useful controlled test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Memory](/architectures/evolution/attention-and-memory/) | Which facts need explicit history, and which can survive a state summary? | Vary dependency distance and repeated distractors; measure retrieval quality, state size, and decoding latency |
| [Visual allocation](/architectures/evolution/multimodal-integration/) | Can the visual budget follow the evidence a question needs? | Compare fine detail tasks at matched image and inference budgets; include questions needing several regions |
| [Feature computation](/architectures/evolution/ffns-and-experts/) | When do richer FFNs or sparse experts justify their complexity? | Match active compute and training budget; separately report total weights, routing, and communication costs |
| [Position](/architectures/evolution/position-and-long-context/) | Which spatial and temporal relationships survive preprocessing? | Change sampling, resolution, and evidence position independently; test whether the answer still depends on the right evidence |
| [Integration](/architectures/models/qwen3-vl/) | Where should visual features enter the decoder? | Hold encoder, data, and training budget fixed; ablate input only versus additional depth connections |

These are proposed tests, not experiments performed by Latent Light.
They describe what to measure, not which model will win.

## What would change our view?

A promising result first enters as an attributed finding under its tested
conditions. A broader recommendation needs relevant comparisons, enough
implementation detail to inspect, and evidence beyond a favorable example.
Negative results matter too: a gain that disappears when budgets are matched
should change the explanation.

Separate a mechanism's plausibility from demonstrated usefulness.
For a proprietary model, public disclosure and plausible reconstruction are
different evidence. An unknown internal detail must not become a fact through
analogy with another paper.

## Add a model when it teaches something

A new [model study](/architectures/models/) earns its place by revealing
a distinct mechanism, an instructive combination, or a clear implementation.
Connect it to the existing design questions and revise any conclusion that
its evidence challenges. A release announcement alone is not a reason to add
another page.
