Latent Light is my personal notebook for exploring AI technology, products and
markets, and human capabilities. I built it for my own learning and share it
with anyone who finds it useful.

With AI extending what we can learn and create, I believe we should develop
both depth and range. Like LeBron James shaping a game across the whole court,
we should see the broader system and contribute wherever we can make a
meaningful difference.

That means understanding how technology works, which problems matter, and how
to turn ideas into useful results. It also means thinking critically, taking
initiative, communicating well, cultivating taste, and caring for the mind
and body behind the work.
