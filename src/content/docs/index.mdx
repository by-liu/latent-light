Latent Light is my personal notebook: a place to explore AI, products, and how we think, learn, and build in the era of AI. I built it for my own learning and share it with anyone who finds it useful.

As AI expands what we can learn and create, I believe we should strive to become more complete players, developing technical depth, product understanding, and our human potential. Like LeBron James shaping a game across the whole court, we should see the broader system and contribute wherever we can make a meaningful difference.

That range takes more than technical knowledge. It means thinking critically, taking initiative, communicating well, and cultivating human taste. It also means caring for the mind and body that make good work possible.

Latent Light is where I explore these ideas and put them into practice. The future is uncertain, but there is plenty worth understanding and plenty we can help shape.
