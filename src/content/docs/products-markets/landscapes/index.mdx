What changes when AI becomes part of a product, rather than the whole pitch?
These landscapes connect capabilities to the people they serve, the work they
do and the businesses built around them.

Large ecosystems, model providers and specialists sit alongside one another.
Start with a visual overview, then use the company explorer to compare products,
operating boundaries, funding and ownership. Each study adds product contrasts,
dated evidence and questions worth investigating. The aim is orientation, not
a leaderboard or an exhaustive directory.

## How to read these maps

**Examples establish a pattern, not its market share.** Categories overlap.
A company can serve consumers, professionals and businesses through different
channels. Its appearances in several studies offer different perspectives,
not additional companies to count.

**Features are not outcomes.** Official product pages describe offers and
provider claims, not independently established learning gains, wellbeing,
reliability or customer economics. Interpretation and proposed evaluation
questions remain separate from those claims.

**Funding is a separate signal.** Explorers give funding and ownership their own
column, apart from product status. Round size, total capital, valuation and
secondary share sales are different measures. A corporate product is not a
separately funded startup; financing alone does not establish product success.

**Read the dates.** The latest funding review is dated **October 11, 2026**.
Each disclosure keeps its event date, and product evidence retains its own
verification dates. Historical rounds are not guaranteed to be the latest;
planned raises and listings are not completed events. Unknown means unverified,
not unfunded.
