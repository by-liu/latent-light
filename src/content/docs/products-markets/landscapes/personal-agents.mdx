“Agent” can mean a shopping recommendation, a recurring alert, a prepared reply
or a completed booking. The useful comparison is **what responsibility the
person delegates, what authority the service receives and how completion is
verified**.

This study includes **23 company groups** and a **separate five-case watchlist**.
Large ecosystem platforms sit alongside specialist services. Some combine personal
and professional use. Useful
boundary comparisons remain explicit rather than being relabeled autonomous.

My working conclusion: a narrow service that reliably prepares the right next
step may be more useful than a broad assistant that needs constant supervision.
That is a product hypothesis, not a market-size forecast or tested ranking.

## Delegation patterns

Anthropic’s distinction between fixed-path workflows and model-directed agents
is useful for architecture. It does not tell us who the customer is, who is
allowed to spend money or whether a human operator finishes the job.[^dir-agent-definition]

Start with three product questions: whose goal is being pursued, what happens
beyond the answer and who controls the consequential step. [Figure 1](#figure-1)
separates levels of responsibility; it is not an official autonomy scale.
NIST likewise treats permissions, monitoring, reversibility and autonomy as
distinct dimensions.[^dir-tool-boundaries]

| Lens | What it does | A useful test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Find and compare options. The user makes the external commitment. |  |  |
| **Watch** | Monitor changes and bring relevant information back. |  |  |
| **Prepare** | Turn scattered context into a draft, briefing or plan. |  |  |
| **Act within a scope** | Change an external system under an explicit task and approval boundary. |  |  |

A separate dimension: AI only or AI plus human operators. Duckbill explicitly describes AI preparation with human execution on every task.

## Product lessons beyond the label

### Big platforms do not have one common autonomy boundary

ChatGPT agent describes permission checks for consequential actions and browser
takeover. Gemini Spark describes opt-in connected apps and checks before major
actions. Alibaba’s January Qwen App announcement separates explicit payment
confirmation, public testing and its invite-only Task Assistant.[^broad-chatgpt][^broad-spark][^broad-qwen]

Apple’s contextual suggestions and Microsoft’s research/analytics features are
useful boundary comparisons, not evidence of unrestricted external execution.
Alexa+ combines supported service actions with forward-looking agentic examples.
Do not infer current access or a universal confirmation policy from the provider’s
name.[^broad-apple][^broad-copilot][^broad-alexa]

**The product lesson:** classify an actual task and its current release. “Agent”
is not a substitute for checking the permission, execution and recovery contract.

### Ohai and Duckbill: reduce the household’s mental load

Ohai connects messy household information to schedules and tasks, with human
support for difficult inputs. Duckbill uses AI preparation and human handling
for personal administration. They make different promises about how work is
completed.[^ohai][^duckbill]

**Why this matters:** human involvement can be part of the product design, not
a failure of the AI. Compare cost and time saved after intake, clarification,
review and follow-up. A hybrid concierge’s price buys service capacity;
it should not be compared with software-only inference costs.

### jo, Town and Littlebird: context is useful only if the controls are clear

These products connect ongoing context to briefings, drafts and routines. The
cited jo profile and Town trust page describe approval for consequential actions.
Littlebird advertises routines and controllable capture, but that does not
establish identical approval enforcement.[^dir-jo-yc][^dir-town-trust][^dir-littlebird-routines]

**Why this matters:** knowing more about someone and having more authority are
different permissions. Inspect the settings for collection, retention, sharing
and action separately. Ask what requires confirmation, what can run in the
background and how a person revokes access.

### Yutori: watching is not the same as acting

Scouts illustrates recurring monitoring; Delegate describes connected-app and
browser work. The changelog includes action capabilities, so Yutori should not
be reduced to alerts alone.[^dir-yutori-product][^dir-yutori-delegate]

**Why this matters:** proactive work needs a stopping rule. A useful monitor
must avoid missing important changes without overwhelming the person with
noise. An actor must also respect limits, confirm sensitive steps and handle
partial completion. The same architecture can require different product controls.

### Mindtrip, Daydream and Phia: discovery can be valuable without auto-buying

Mindtrip offers booking experiences, Daydream sends shoppers toward partner
purchases, and Phia combines price watching with purchase support. None of those
facts establishes unrestricted purchasing on a person’s behalf.[^dir-mindtrip-flights][^dir-mindtrip-stays][^daydream][^dir-phia-product]

**Why this matters:** the final action boundary is part of the product. The
assistant can do substantial useful work while leaving the decision and payment
to the user. Evaluate constraints, total price, disclosed incentives and the
remaining effort, rather than rewarding automation for its own sake.

### Macaron, Second Me and creative agents: different meanings of delegation

Macaron’s memory-informed tools and connected actions differ from Second Me’s
social representation. Flowith, SEELE and YouMind focus on multi-step creative
or research artifacts. Publishing a game is execution within an artifact
workflow, not evidence that the service safely administers bank or travel accounts.[^dir-macaron][^dir-secondme][^dir-flowith][^dir-seele-agent][^youmind]

**Why this matters:** a broad agent count hides differences in authority and
outcomes. Judge a research artifact by provenance and editing quality; judge a
social representative by faithfulness and sharing boundaries; judge an account
action by permission and verified state change.

## How to evaluate an agent

A plausible final answer is not enough when external state can change. Use a
bounded task set with explicit starting conditions, permitted actions and a
definition of completion. NIST’s evaluation work motivates testing behavior
and tool boundaries rather than relying on a label.[^dir-eval-probes]

| Dimension | What to inspect | Example failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome | Evidence that the intended state changed | A booking claim without a confirmed reservation |
| Constraint fidelity | Budget, dates, recipients and user preferences | The right item purchased for the wrong delivery date |
| Authority | Allowed tools, permissions and approval enforcement | A consequential action taken without required confirmation |
| Oversight burden | Setup, review, interruptions and correction | The person checks every step and saves no time |
| Recovery | Retry rules, duplicate prevention, rollback and escalation | A retry sends a second message or buys twice |
| Privacy | Necessary data, retention, sharing and revocation | Private context copied into an unrelated service |
| Economics | Cost per completed task, including retries and human work | Cheap inference with expensive supervision |

This is my proposed evaluation checklist, not a scorecard collected on these
companies. A completion rate is meaningful only with the task distribution and
approval policy stated. “No errors” in a few demonstrations is not a reliability
estimate.

## Scope and open questions

The directory includes household help, personal assistants, web delegation,
travel, shopping, creative work, social representation, learning and a
non-discretionary finance comparator. It is not an inventory of every general
assistant, workplace tool or merchant bot. Presence here is not a recommendation
to grant access to financial or personal accounts.

The strongest next evidence would be independently observed completion,
constraint violations, intervention burden and recovery under realistic failures.
Public approval language is useful, but enforcement still needs testing. Also
ask what happens when a service is discontinued, a connector changes or a person
wants to export their history.
